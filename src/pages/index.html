Learning path

4 stages, 12 levels

From moving the pieces to thinking like an international player. Each stage is 12 hours, online or onsite.

  1. 1Stage 1 · Lv. 1–4

    Fun Basic Chess

    Basic rules, piece movement, and simple planning

    12 hours

  2. 2Stage 2 · Lv. 5–8

    Ready to Tournament

    Game analysis, strategy, and preparation for competitive play

    12 hours

  3. 3Stage 3 · Lv. 9–10

    Think Like FM

    Thinking ahead, adapting during play, and tournament techniques

    12 hours

  4. 4Stage 4 · Lv. 11–12

    Move Like IM

    Strategic thinking, tactics, and preparation for international-level play

    12 hours
