Our story

From the tournament board
to the teaching table.

CapyChess School was founded by three FIDE Masters who have represented the Thailand and Taiwan national teams. Each founder brings more than 10 years of chess teaching experience, combining competitive expertise with a deep understanding of how to help young players learn and develop.
